FIZZ: the TAP ape (proposal)

SHIFT + B6 taps the tempo. The metronome goes; a breakdancing ape takes the right of the screen and jumps to a new dance pose on every tap (a 120 ms snap with a hop, motion streaks, a burst of sparkles, the boombox pumping). Once two taps give a tempo, it grooves on the beat between taps. "TAP", the BPM and the tap dots stay; the BPM moves to the top left, over the boombox, so the ape's raised fists never cross it.

Try it

Click the screen or press space to tap (the BPM is the firmware's: the average of the last four taps). Stop for 2 s: the dots clear, the ape strikes its ta-da and the screen closes. Tap faster than 180 BPM for the easter egg.

The poses

Twelve dance poses and the bench pose for an external clock. Each is a set of joint angles on one skeleton (torso, head, two arms and two legs, two segments each); the firmware tweens the angles between poses.
