FIZZ: the TAP ape (proposal, round 2)

SHIFT + B6 taps the tempo. The metronome goes; the owner's breakdancing gorilla takes the screen, its boombox on the floor at the left, and jumps to the next of its twelve poses on every tap (a hop, a flash, a ghost of the pose it left, sparkles, the boombox pumping). Once two taps give a tempo, it grooves on the beat between taps. "TAP", the BPM and the tap dots stay: the BPM sits top left at 44 px with "BPM" under it, so the bigger ape has the room. The screen stays up 2.8 s after the last tap: the ape strikes its finale at 2 s.

Try it

Click the screen or press space to tap (the BPM is the firmware's: the average of the last four taps). Stop: the dots clear at 2 s and the ape kneels, fist up; the screen closes at 2.8 s. Tap faster than 180 BPM for the easter egg.
