FIZZ: MIDI FX groups (mock-up)

Five shared groups of eight slots, as on the OMX-27. Each slot holds one of ten MIDI effects, or nothing. A track sends its notes through one group (or OFF); two tracks may share a group, and then their notes meet in one chain (one arp plays both tracks' notes). Notes pass the slots in order, slot 1 → slot 8 → the voices. SHIFT + ARP opens the MIDI FX screen on the selected track's group; HOME or a tap of F1 closes it. Under each screen are its keys: filled = held, outlined = lit, faint = dim, dashed = blinking. The LEDs are one colour, so the keys canvas uses mint for the MIDI FX screen's lights.

The ARP's pages

1 PATTERN, RATE, RANGE, CHANCE · 2 MODE (ON / 1-ST / ONCE / HOLD), GATE, OCT DIST, QUANT · 3 the MOD lane · 4 the TRANSPOSE lane. The 17 patterns: UP, DN, UPDN, DNUP, U&D, D&U, CON, DIV, C-V, HI 1, HI 2, LO 1, LO 2, RAND, ROTH, RONC, ASP.

Open points (for the owner)

  • ARP's light would carry two things: FILL (ARP held, lit) and the arp's on / off (ARP tapped). Suggestion: lit = arp on, blinking fast while FILL is held.
  • On the arp's lanes (pages 3, 4) B1..B10 are values and the white keys are steps, so B1..B8 cannot select a slot, B10 / B11 do nothing and the keys cannot play. SELECT (or HOME) is the way out, as on the OMX.
  • SHIFT + W (a random lane value) means SHIFT held there shows no SHIFT map (as in the sound editor, P1.5z).
  • PRESETS chooses the slot's type here; everywhere else (the sound editor too) it opens the library browser.
  • The bypassed slot's key: drawn blinking here (B10 dark). A slot key that is the selected one and bypassed blinks bright; another bypassed slot blinks dim.
