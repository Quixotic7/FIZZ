FIZZ: saving and the sound pool (P1.5a mock-up)

Version 2, after the owner's answers. The project is written by itself; SAVE stores the track's sound in your LIBRARY (shared by every project) and F2 then adds it to the project's POOL; one sounds page browses both (B9). Single clicks: a press again confirms, nothing is held to confirm. Under each screen are the FM-1's keys: a filled key is held, an outlined key is lit, a dashed one blinks. The library is champagne, the pool lime, a track its own colour.
