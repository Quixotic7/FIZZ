FIZZ: the PIANO ROLL's hold modes as a 4 × 4 grid (mock-ups, 2026-11-13)

The owner: some hold modes "are impossible to read, for example functions results in super small icons. The sound locks is also confusing." For COND, FUNC, TRIG, SOUND and SLIDE the page's 16 steps become 16 boxes, four to a row (a row is a beat). Left: the firmware today (its own render). Right: the proposal, one or two takes. The page is the same in every screen: DRUMS on A01 playing (the playhead in step 7), the ALGORITHM cursor on step 11.

One box language for all five: an empty step is a dark outline; a trig on the track's value a grey box with the value grey; a step with its own value a box tinted and outlined in the mode's colour with the value big. The playhead is a red bar along the box's foot (the box flashes white as the step plays, as STEPS' cells do); the cursor / held step is outlined white, as in STEPS and EDIT. The top line and page dashes are the screen's own.
