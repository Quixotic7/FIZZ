FIZZ: track overview and MIDI FX routing (mock-ups, 2026-11-10)

From a reviewer's feedback: "a home screen with an overview of all the tracks" and "what gets sent where" in MIDI FX. Shown on A02 (the band) playing, step 7 of page 1, BASS selected, PLUCK muted.

1. The overview: four takes

Any take would be the fifth view in SHIFT + HOME's cycle (PIANO ROLL, STEPS, EDIT, GEEK OUT, OVERVIEW), kept like the others. On it, as in the mixer: B1 … B6 tapped selects a track, held mutes it, double-tapped solos it; the white keys are the selected track's steps; SELECT turns the page.

2. MIDI FX routing

ALGORITHM on the ARP screen already shows the five groups; turning it now draws every track wired to its group (today it shows only the selected track's square). The mixer stays as it is.
