FIZZ: MIDI OUT and the MIDI track (mock-up round 1, 2026-10-27)

Track settings page 3 gets KNOB 3 ENGINE (FM TONE or MIDI) and KNOB 4 MIDI OUT (OFF, or a channel 1 … 16, over USB). A MIDI track has no sound: EDIT opens seven pages of four CCs (SELECT or B1 … B7 picks the page). Each bar is a value in its knob's colour, sent as you turn it; the CC number sits under it. A slot you never touched is an empty dashed bar and sends nothing. SHIFT + a knob picks that slot's CC number. Hold a step and turn: a p-lock, sent as that step plays.
