FIZZ: MASTER as two heads (mock-up, 2026-10-26)

The master filter as two profiles face to face. The left head is the LOW CUT (KNOB 1, blue, solid), the right head the HIGH CUT (KNOB 2, green, striped); each slides along the frequency axis as its knob turns, its nose at its cutoff, so the gap between the faces is what passes. The filter off: both grey. SLOPE: the stripes closer (12 dB).
