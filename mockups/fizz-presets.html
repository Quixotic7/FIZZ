FIZZ: the Preset Library and the Sound Pool (proposal)

Three things, one job each. The track's sound is what a track plays (saved with its pattern, edited in EDIT). The Preset Library holds every preset: FACTORY (built in) and MINE (saved and imported), shared by every project. The Sound Pool is the project's own 128 sounds that steps lock. PRESETS browses the library (the track plays what you turn to; F2 loads, F1 cancels); a step + PRESETS chooses from the pool; SAVE saves the track's sound as a preset. Under each screen: the keys (filled = held, outlined = lit, dashed = blinking).
