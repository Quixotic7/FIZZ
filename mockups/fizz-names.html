FIZZ: typing names on the keybed (proposal)

The keybed becomes a typewriter: the 11 black keys are A..K, the 16 white keys L..Z and SPACE (reading order: the top row, then the bottom row), drawn on the screen with a letter on every key. The suggested name starts selected, so the first key typed replaces it. SHIFT gives digits and signs, PRESETS name ideas, ALGORITHM the cursor, F1 deletes, SAVE confirms, HOME cancels.
