FIZZ: FX ROUTING (mock-up, 2026-10-26)

After ChoralRoot 0.27's Routing page. KNOB 1 the mode, PARALLEL (today's buses) or CHAIN (one path, the OEM way). Hold a block's own page key (B4 CHORUS, B2 DELAY, B3 REVERB, B5 FILTER) and it lifts; KNOB 1 then moves it a place a detent; let go and it lands. In PARALLEL the filter alone moves, before or after the buses. In CHAIN KNOB 2 .. 4 are each effect's amount (the tracks' sends rest); in PARALLEL they are dim (the sends rule). No hint text: the four keys' LEDs glow, the held one lit.
