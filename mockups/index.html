QUADRILLE: an FM TONE step sequencer for the FM-1

Mock-ups for PLAN.md. Each screen is a 240 x 240 canvas drawn pixel for pixel and shown here at 2x (nearest neighbour), so what you see is what the panel would show. Two directions side by side: MOD (ChoralRoot's look: black, heavy cream type, flat blocks, role colours) and RISO (new: a print look, paper and fluorescent inks, halftone dots, a little misregistration). The example project: pattern A03, 122 BPM, eight tracks (1 DRUMS with a sound lock on every step, 2 BASS mono, 3 CHORDS, 4 LEAD, 5 PLUCK, 6 PAD, 7 BELL muted, 8 NOISE), sixteen voices all in use. A third direction, LINE (after the OP-1's screens, twenty of them, animated), is on its own page: line.html.
