FIZZ: Geek Out (mock-up)

SHIFT + HOME cycles PIANO ROLL → STEPS → EDIT → METERS → NUMBERS → VOICES → SCOPE. The four pages share one header: the page's name, the transport (a red dot beating while playing, a grey square stopped) and four little pictures of the pages top right, the one on screen lit. All four run on the same simulated performance: eight tracks at 122 BPM through the 8-voice pool, 13 s playing then 5 s stopped; the chord, the pad and the arp meet and steal voices; the screen's frame rate dips once (a heavy redraw). Yellow means trouble on these pages (a steal, a late half, a slow frame, a load over 60 %, a crash), so a glance says whether all is well; orange over 85 %.

Open questions (for the owner)

  • Yellow for trouble. The knob colours mean nothing here (no knob edits a value on these pages), so the meters go green → yellow → orange with the load, and every warning (steal, late, slow frame, crash) is yellow. Track 6 is yellow too, so a steal is also a shape (a spark of rays), never only the colour. Recommendation: keep yellow plus the spark, and keep red for the playhead and recording only.
  • Do the knobs do anything here? Recommendation: no; SHIFT + B11 (as the clear gesture elsewhere) resets the counters (steals, late halves, the longest ISR and lock) so a test can start from zero. Everything else plays as on the step view.
  • The scope's window. Recommendation: about three cycles of the loudest track's lowest note, locked to its rising zero crossing so the line stands still on a held note (as ChoralRoot's); the free-running look is the fallback if the pitch is not known cheaply.
