FIZZ: the SHIFT screen, track first (mock-up, 2026-11-10)

Holding SHIFT (GLO) now says which track you are on before anything else: its number, kind and name big in its colour, and the six tracks beside it (SELECT turns them while SHIFT is held). The shortcuts become tiles, each with its control and its value now; the black keys' strip stays at the foot. The same screen shows over every page.
