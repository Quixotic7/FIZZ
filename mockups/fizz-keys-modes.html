FIZZ: the white keys by mode, take 2 (mock-up, 2026-11-12)

Each hold mode keeps its own screen. The heading's labels (TRACK, STEP n, the mode, DEFAULT, NOTE's scale) give way to the keyboard page's row of the page's 16 steps (the trigs, the selected step outlined, the playhead); the value keeps its corner, just under the row. No page is shown: turning SELECT flashes the page's black key, and so it does wherever SELECT turns pages. In LIVE (no step selected) the screens stay as they are now. With a step selected, white keys held + B11 put the step's value for the mode back to the track's. The lock's frame goes round the keys. The key LEDs under each screen show the step's value as ALGORITHM moves through the steps.
