QUADRILLE: the LINE screens

A third direction for PLAN.md, after the OP-1's screens: black, thin lines with round ends, one little drawing per screen, and colour means a knob: whatever KNOB 1 turns is blue, KNOB 2 green, KNOB 3 white, KNOB 4 orange, on every screen, and every value is a part of the drawing, so there are no rows of numbers: turn a knob and its part of the drawing moves. A knob whose value the screen does not draw takes the whole screen while it turns and hands it back a second later (screens 21..25). Red is only the playhead and recording. SELECT always turns what KNOB 1 turns (the big value). Each canvas is the 240 x 240 panel at 2x and runs live at 122 BPM. The example project as in the other directions: pattern A03, eight tracks (1 DRUMS with a sound lock on every step, 2 BASS, 3 CHORDS, 4 LEAD, 5 PLUCK, 6 PAD, 7 BELL muted, 8 NOISE).

The strip of eleven little keys at the foot of a held step's screens is the keybed's black keys (3 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 1): the lit one is the field on screen, an outlined one holds a value.
