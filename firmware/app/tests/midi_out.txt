# FIZZ MIDI (c), MIDI OUT on the unit's build (research/FIZZ-MIDI-PLAN.md §4): the port sends the core's out queue to
# USB from the audio interrupt, right after its block (app.c app_render, fz_port.c fz_midi_out). On the demo's A02:
# track 1's MIDI OUT 3 (track settings page 3, KNOB 4), its notes out on channel 3 as it plays; the clock and the
# transport; a live key; the knobs' CCs on the track's MIDI CH (1); a CC in not echoed.
#   firmware/build.py --host, then firmware/app/build/host/fizz_host firmware/app/tests/midi_out.txt OUT
expect fz_up 1
tap HOME
wait 300
# track settings page 3: MIDI OUT 3
tap SEL
wait 100
turn SELECT 2
wait 100
turn K4 3
wait 300
shot midi-out-3.png
expect midi_out 0
tap HOME
wait 100
out
# PLAY: START, the clock, track 1's notes on channel 3; STOP: STOP and their OFFs
tap PLAY
wait 2000
expect_out USB FA 00 00
expect clocks_out >70
out
tap PLAY
wait 100
expect_out USB FC 00 00
expect_no_out USB 90 24 64
out
# a live key on the plain keyboard (REC tapped): C4 out on 3, and its OFF
tap REC
wait 300
key 7 down 100
wait 100
expect_out USB 92 3C 64
key 7 up
wait 100
expect_out USB 82 3C 00
tap REC
wait 300
out
# EDIT's KNOB 1 (the first page's RATIO C): its CC 25 on track 1's MIDI CH (1), as its value moves
tap EDIT
wait 300
turn K1 -12
wait 200
expect_out USB B0 19 00
out
tap HOME
wait 100
# a CC in (FREQ, channel 1) is not sent back
midi B0 4A 20
wait 50
expect_no_out USB B0 4A 20
out
