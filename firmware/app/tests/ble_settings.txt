# The Bluetooth package (firmware/build.py --ble --host: fizz-ble_host): SETTINGS page 2 (GLO + SEL, then SELECT):
# BLUETOOTH ON / OFF and the radio's state, read-only. OFF switches the radio off (app.c fz_ble_look:
# plat_ble_enable), ON on again; the state follows a computer connecting. Shots of the page for the owner.
#   firmware/app/build/host/fizz-ble_host firmware/app/tests/ble_settings.txt OUT
expect fz_up 1
wait 1500
expect ble 2
press GLO
tap SEL
release GLO
wait 300
shot settings-p1.png
turn SELECT 1
wait 300
shot settings-p2-waiting.png
ble connect
wait 300
shot settings-p2-connected.png
# BLUETOOTH OFF: the radio off, the page says so
turn K1 -1
wait 300
expect ble 0
shot settings-p2-off.png
# and ON again: the radio starts
turn K1 1
wait 1500
expect ble 2
shot settings-p2-on.png
turn SELECT -1
wait 300
shot settings-p1-again.png
