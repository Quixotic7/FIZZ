Digitone II harvest

FIZZ, the FM sequencer for the M-VAVE FM-1, can load Digitone II presets. A few of their settings can only be learned from a real Digitone II. This page asks yours, mostly by itself, in about 20 minutes, and makes a zip for you to send us. Thank you!

1. Before you start

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, with the Digitone II connected by USB.
  2. On the Digitone II, start a new, empty project. Pick a track (track 1 is fine) and give it an INIT FM TONE sound.
  3. Stop the sequencer, and make sure live recording is off. Otherwise every change would be recorded into the pattern.
  4. In SETTINGS > MIDI CONFIG, let USB receive notes and CC/NRPN. Check which MIDI channel the track listens on (by default, track 1 is channel 1).
  5. To record the audio, the Digitone II's USB audio must reach the computer. Its USB audio input appears in the list below.

The wizard only changes that one track's sound and never saves anything. When it is done, load another project without saving, or reload this one.
