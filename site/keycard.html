FIZZ key card

The FM-1's panel with FIZZ's name for each control (the printed label small above it), and the moves behind a hold or a second press.

Hidden moves

Views

SHIFT + HOME
next view: PIANO ROLL, STEPS, EDIT, GEEK OUT, OVERVIEW
HOME
back to your view, from anywhere
MIX (ENV)
the mixer; again: the sends
FX
the effects; held + ALGORITHM: choose the track
TRACKS (SEL)
tap: the track settings (ENGINE, MIDI OUT on page 3); held: the tracks (mute, solo)
PATTERNS (SEQ)
held: the patterns; tapped: TOOLS
F3
OCT− + OCT+, or PLAY held: tempo, speed, the page's start and end

Steps

a white key held
edit that step: the black key picks what (NOTE, VEL, LEN, MICRO …), the knobs set it
LATCH (LFO)
the step latch: edit steps without holding them (the blue frame); again or HOME: off
ALGORITHM
the step cursor on the page (1 … 16); fully left: none
B11 held
B1 … B8 the pages (as F1); white keys select steps; + F1 clears them; a click: the latch
REC
the white keys play notes in the track's scale; again: steps
REC, B11 held
white keys select steps; let go: each chord you play goes into them; ALGORITHM fully left or LFO: live
F1 + W / F2 + W
copy / move steps
SHIFT + F1
undo

Sounds and MIDI FX

PRESETS
browse presets; F2 loads, F1 or HOME puts the old sound back
EDIT, then B6 B7 B8
the sound's LFO 1, 2, 3
MIDI FX (ARP)
the track's MIDI FX group (arp, chord …); ALGORITHM: which group
ARP held / SHIFT + ARP
the track's MIDI FX off, and on again
SOUNDS (SAVE)
save the track's sound as a preset; SHIFT + SAVE: projects
