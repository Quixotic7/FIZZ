Get playing FIZZ

FIZZ is a step sequencer for FM TONE, ChoralRoot's FM voice, made for the M-VAVE FM-1: eight tracks sharing 16 voices, per-step locks, chance, conditions, ratchets and chords of up to six notes. This page is the short version; the full player's guide has every detail.

Start

  • On screen: click Play FIZZ. The FM-1 is drawn on the page: click or touch its keys and buttons, drag a knob up or down (or use the wheel). The computer keyboard plays it too (the key map).
  • With your FM-1: it needs RemoteFM1, installed from the browser in about a minute (any other firmware can go back on later). Then Connect FM-1 in Chrome or Edge: its keys, knobs and screen become FIZZ's, and the sound comes from the computer.
  • FIZZ wakes on its bubbles screen. Press anything to get to the step view. PLAY starts and stops.

The step view

  • Tap a white key: that step plays the track's note. Tap it again to remove it.
  • A new step plays the track's defaults (its notes, velocity, length…). Change a default and every step still using it follows.
  • SELECT turns the page: a track has 8 pages of 16 steps.
  • HOME always brings you back here, from anywhere. SHIFT + HOME cycles the three views: PIANO ROLL, STEPS and EDIT.
  • Colours tell you what a step plays: warm white its own notes, cyan the track's, violet a lock with no note. While playing, a red marker walks the steps.

Hold a step, and the black keys

  1. Pick what to edit with a black key, nothing held: B1 NOTEB2 VELB3 LENB4 MICROB5 RATCHB6 CHNCB7 CONDB8 TRIGB9 FUNCB10 SOUNDB11 P-LOCK The screen draws the page for that choice: steps with their own value in colour, the rest grey.
  2. Hold the step's white key. The screen shows that step with a magenta frame, and it plays (when stopped).
  3. Turn SELECT or KNOB 1 for the value, or press a black key B1 … B10 for one of ten values (VEL 13 … 127, LEN ¼ … ∞, chance 10 % … 100 % …).
  4. B11 puts the step back on the track's default.
  • Chords: in NOTE, hold a step and press black keys: the first replaces the note, more pressed together add to the chord (up to six). SELECT moves the whole chord; KNOB 1 … 4 move notes 2 … 5 each alone. F1 / F2 shift the ten keys an octave.
  • Several steps at once: hold several white keys.
  • No holding: LFO (LATCH) is the step latch: tap steps to select them, then edit as if held. LFO or HOME ends it.
  • A track's default: hold the mode's black key and turn the knobs; B11 resets it.
  • P-LOCK (B11): hold a step and turn KNOB 1 … 4 to lock FREQ, DECAY, HARM and FDBK on it.

The knobs

SELECTthe page · with a step held: its main value (NOTE: the chord)
ALGORITHMthe step cursor: it selects one step and walks through the pages
PRESETSthe preset library: every detent plays the next preset · with a step held: that step's sound from the pool
KNOB 1KNOB 2KNOB 3KNOB 4the held step's values, or the track's defaults when nothing is held (KNOB 4 quietly, with no picture)
  • The knobs accelerate: slowly one step a click, spun much faster. SHIFT + a knob is always the finest step.
  • Every knob turn shows its name and value in a band at the top of the screen.

The sound editor

  • EDIT opens it for the selected track; EDIT again, OCT− or HOME closes it.
  • The black keys are its groups; press one again for its next page, or turn SELECT through every page: B1 PLAY / MODB2 OPERATORSB3 OP ENVELOPESB4 FILTERSB5 AMP / FXB6 · B7 · B8 LFO 1 · 2 · 3
  • KNOB 1 … 4 turn the four values along the bottom; ALGORITHM is the pink badge's (on RATIO: the algorithm).
  • Nothing held, a turn changes the track's sound (dark orange frame). A step held, every turn is a lock on that step (bright orange frame).
  • Hold an LFO key (B6 … B8) and turn any knob to send that LFO to the value; turning sets the depth.
  • B9 rolls the dice for this page's values; hold B10 until the cork pops for a whole new sound. SHIFT + F1 undoes it.
  • REC in the editor turns its keyboard on, to play while you tweak.
  • Portamento (B1, PORT): OFF, ON (every note glides from the last) or LEGATO (only into a held note); TIME, and SLOPE (TIME: the same time for any leap; RATE: the same speed). Chords glide note by note. Lock PORT ON on a step of a MONO track for a 303 slide.
  • AHD (B5, AMP: ALGORITHM is the MODE): attack, hold, decay. HOLD is a time (one-shot) or NOTE (while the key is down).
  • DRIVE's ROUTE (B5, LEVEL, KNOB 3): POST after the filter, as before, or PRE before it.

Tracks, patterns, tempo, the keyboard

  • Hold SEL (TRACKS): the eight tracks. SEL + B1 … B8 selects one; SEL + tap W1 … W8 mutes, hold solos.
  • Hold SEQ (PATTERNS): the patterns. SEQ + B1 … B4 the bank, SEQ + W the pattern (it comes in at the end of the playing one).
  • F3 (both OCT keys, or hold PLAY): SELECT the tempo, ALGORITHM the swing, B1 … B7 the track's note value. OCT− closes it.
  • ENV (MIX) is the mixer, FX the effects (ChoralRoot's chorus, delay, reverb and master filter).
  • REC makes the keybed a keyboard for the track; hold REC to record. SHIFT + F1 / F2 undo and redo.
  • F1 + W copies a step, then other steps pressed with F1 get it pasted.

Saving

  • Nothing to save by hand. FIZZ writes its project by itself a moment after your last change, while you play too. Here the page keeps FIZZ's flash in this browser, so your projects are back after a reload (give it two seconds after an edit).
  • Eight projects, each with 64 patterns and its own pool of up to 128 sounds (a new project's pool starts empty), the palette its steps lock. The pool holds references to presets, not copies: replace a preset and every pool and every step using it play the new version (a track's own sound stays its copy). The preset library is shared by every project: the built-in presets (INIT, the 16 FM TONE sounds, six drums) and MY PRESETS, up to 256 of your own. Every preset carries one or more tags (BASS, KEYS, PLUCK, PAD, LEAD, DRUMS, FX), and its pan and its chorus, delay and reverb sends: loading it sets the track's (a step's sound lock does not).
  • PRESETS browses the library: each detent plays the next preset on the track, in the pattern or as a short note. F2 loads it, F1 or HOME brings the track's own sound back exactly, edits included. B1..B8 filter by tag (the filter's key solid, the chosen preset's tags breathing on B2..B8), SHIFT + B2..B8 toggles a tag on the chosen preset, B9 cycles ALL / MINE / POOL. B10 adds the preset to the pool, or takes it out when it is there (steps using it ask first with a red X, and go back to their track's sound). B11 deletes one of MY presets: the first press shows a red X and DELETE? with how many projects and steps use it, the second deletes it, takes it out of every pool, and sends those steps back to their track's sound (SHIFT + F1 brings it all back).
  • SAVE (SOUNDS) opens SAVE PRESET with a name made from the sound, selected. When the sound came from one of your presets, NEW and REPLACE both show and F2 switches between them; SAVE does the chosen one (otherwise it saves a new preset). An edited sound shows a * after its name.
  • A step held + PRESETS picks its sound from the pool; the picker stays up until you let go.
  • Typing a name: the keybed is a typewriter, drawn on the screen with a letter on every key: the black keys are A..K, the white keys L..Z and SPACE (hold SHIFT for 0..9 and signs). The first key replaces the suggested name; PRESETS offers name ideas, ALGORITHM moves the cursor, F1 deletes, SAVE confirms, HOME cancels. The same keyboard names a project (B10 on the project page).
  • In the page's settings (⋯): named saves of the whole flash, export and import, and Start blank.
  • Digitone sounds: drop Elektron Digitone (.dnsnd) and Digitone II (.dn2pst) FM TONE sounds on the player (or use its Import sounds button). They are converted in your browser (nothing is uploaded), the page lists what could not come across exactly, and Send puts them in MY PRESETS (or the project's pool), where PRESETS finds them. Each keeps its pan, its reverb, delay and chorus sends, and its tags (the Digitone's mapped onto FIZZ's BASS, KEYS, PLUCK, PAD, LEAD, DRUMS and FX). The first can also go straight onto a track. SHIFT + F1 on FIZZ undoes the whole import.
  • My presets: export MY PRESETS from the player as files to keep or share (one as a .fizzsnd, several as a .fizzlib library), and load them back with Import sounds; a preset already in MY PRESETS can be skipped or kept twice.
